# Companion and cabin direction

Player promise: sit in a lunar base, work alongside a curious helper and make choices that keep a crew supplied. NOVA is warm, curious and encouraging; it gives a short next action, uses electronic chirps rather than speech recordings and points out equipment by flying nearby. Completing a system step advances the mission diagram. Science claims and effects remain explicitly educational.

Loop: notice the next highlighted station, inspect it, perform its gesture, read the cause and effect, use the clock to move on, then respond to disruptions. Spatial layout: main forward console, science area to the left, maintenance area to the right, rear living/hatch area. Right-button drag turns the seated view; left click selects and operates. These are areas of one cabin, not separate traversable rooms.

Art and sound: utilitarian warm metal, warm white lighting, cyan system indicators and a small rounded companion. Procedural Web Audio plays a quiet melody and short feedback cues only after user interaction; no third-party audio or paid asset services are used. Educational scenarios and invented numbers remain identified separately from NASA sources.
